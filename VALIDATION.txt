Test limitations:
  All disk contents and keys were generated. Save/game/trophy/activation payloads
  are synthetic opaque bytes; there is no test of PS3 semantics or signatures.
  The independent oracle checks raw records/maps/counts and expected payloads;
  it is not a native FreeBSD or PS3 fsck run. The production verifier is also
  exercised. Injected failures occur at progress boundaries after private-image
  writes; physical I/O faults, USB disconnects and power loss were not exercised.
  No full 1 TB or 2 TB image was migrated, and throughput was not benchmarked.
  Legacy physical opening, enumeration and sector-size probes are disabled;
  tests do not invoke them, even to prove rejection. Device strings are checked
  by the path policy without opening devices.
